Luxury Residences in Manpada, Thane

Discover an exclusive luxury residential development in Manpada, Thane, spread across approximately 18 acres of pristine surroundings. Nestled in the embrace of Sanjay Gandhi National Park, the project offers a premium lifestyle surrounded by verdant hills, lush greenery, and natural beauty.

The development features thoughtfully planned 2 Bed, 3 Bed Signature, 4 Bed Signature, and 4 Bed Royale residences, with carpet areas ranging from 750+ sq. ft. to 2,000+ sq. ft., designed to offer spacious living, comfort, and contemporary elegance.

Experience a premium lifestyle with proposed amenities, including a swimming pool, premium clubhouse, gymnasium, jogging track, landscaped recreational spaces, rooftop lounges, and dedicated family and children's recreation areas, creating an enriching environment for leisure and wellness.

Strategically located in Manpada, Thane, the project offers access to Ghodbunder Road, Hiranandani Estate, and established social infrastructure. With EOI prices starting from ₹1.89 Cr.++*, along with upcoming connectivity developments such as the Thane–Borivali Twin Tunnel, Thane–Wadala Metro, Thane Coastal Road, and planned Bullet Train station, it presents an exclusive residential opportunity where luxury meets nature.

About Godrej Properties

Godrej Group, established in 1897 during India's Swadeshi movement, has evolved into a global powerhouse with a presence in consumer goods, real estate, appliances, and agriculture, among others. Founded by Ardeshir Godrej, the company now serves over 1.1 billion consumers worldwide and generates more than USD 6 billion in revenue. Godrej Properties, the real estate arm established in 1990, combines the group's legacy of innovation, sustainability, and excellence, with a focus on cutting-edge design and technology.

The company operates across 12 cities in India, managing 92 projects with a saleable area of around 200 million square feet. A public company since 2010, Godrej Properties has received over 400 awards and is committed to environmental, social, and governance initiatives. Core regions of focus include the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, NCR, Pune, and Bengaluru, with a goal of fostering joy through its developments.
